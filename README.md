## Project Overview

**Movie_review_sentiment_prediction is an NLP Project which aims to explore various Machine Learning algorithms to best predict the sentiment of movies from their reviews. The best model is determined by analyzing their performance metrics.

## Deployment

**The model can be further developed into an application through streamlit deployment on AWS. This will offer a user friendly application that will enable movie lovers write their reviews and movie directors/producers to receive feedback of sentiments leading to the following benefits.

## Benefits

  - Crafting targeted marketing campaigns: Knowing what resonates with viewers can allow producers to tailor their messaging, attracting the right audience and maximizing impact.
  - Fine-tuning film production: Sentiment analysis can inform script revisions, character development, and even genre adjustments, ensuring the final product truly connects with its intended audience.
  - Building stronger audience relationships: By actively listening to and understanding their viewers' emotions, studios can foster a sense of community and trust, solidifying long-term loyalty.
